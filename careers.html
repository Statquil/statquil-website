Do the work that keeps everything else running.

01 / The team

Small teams. Senior work. No theatre.

Statquil is 25+ practitioners across data engineering, database administration, cloud infrastructure and data center operations. We are deliberately small, and we intend to stay that way for a while.

That means the person who designs a platform is close enough to the people who run it, and close enough to the client, to hear when something is wrong. It also means there is nowhere to hide. We consider that a feature.

03 / What we offer

Conditions for good work.

Not a list of perks. The things that decide whether an engineer can do their best work here.

Senior work, not tickets.

Engineers spend their time on architecture, delivery and hard problems, not queue clearing.

Depth over volume.

We take on fewer engagements so the people on them can do the work properly.

Hybrid by default.

Office time in Hyderabad for collaboration, focused time at home for the work that needs it.

Certification support.

Relevant cloud, database and data engineering certifications are funded and planned into your year.

Small teams.

You will know everyone you work with, including both founders.

A written culture.

Decisions are documented. Expectations are explicit. Nobody is managed by hallway memory.

04 / Hiring

A process without games.

Four steps, clear timelines and no take home assignment longer than two hours.

  1. 01

    Application review

    Send a CV or a note about relevant work. We reply within five working days.

  2. 02

    Conversation

    Thirty minutes on your experience and what you want next. No trick questions.

  3. 03

    Craft

    A technical conversation and a short practical exercise, scoped to two hours.

  4. 04

    Offer

    A written offer with clear expectations and a start date that respects your notice period.

05 / Equal opportunity

Capability and evidence. Nothing else.

We assess candidates on the work they can do and how they think. Background, gender, age, religion, disability and family status are not part of the decision.

If you need an adjustment at any stage of the process, tell us. It is a reasonable request, not a mark against you, and we will make it.

We keep applications on file for twelve months unless you ask us not to. See the privacy policy for how candidate data is handled.
