The company

A company built around the unglamorous layer.

Statquil Technologies was founded in Hyderabad in 2026 to do one thing properly: build and run the data and infrastructure systems that businesses rely on every day.

01 / Our story

We started with a narrow brief: make the foundation reliable, then leave it strong enough to run without us.

Statquil was founded by Dinesh Munipally and Umarani around a gap they kept seeing. Plenty of data tools, very few teams willing to take end to end responsibility for the platform underneath them.

That responsibility covers architecture, engineering and operations. It means designing for the people who will carry the pager, documenting decisions while they are fresh and measuring success in uptime, reconciliation and cost.

Today the team is 25+ practitioners across data engineering, database administration, cloud infrastructure and data center operations. We have delivered more than 10 engagements for organizations in financial services, healthcare, manufacturing and consumer businesses.

We stay deliberately small so senior people remain close to the work. The person who designs the platform is the person who answers questions about it a year later. That is the model, and we intend to keep it.

03 / What we value

Four commitments, applied to every engagement.

They shape who we hire, how we scope and what we are willing to sign our names to.

01

Clarity over cleverness.

The simplest architecture that meets the requirement wins. Complexity has to earn its place, in writing.

02

Own the outcome.

An engagement is not finished when the code ships. It is finished when the platform runs without us in the room.

03

Write it down.

Decisions, assumptions and limits belong in the record. Documentation is how a team scales judgment.

04

Leave it better.

Every handover should leave the client team more capable than when we arrived. That is the test that matters.

04 / Founders

Two founders. One standard.

Both founders stay involved in delivery. Escalation does not disappear into an account layer, and the answer to a hard question is never a committee.

Dinesh Munipally

Founder

Dinesh sets the technical direction of the company. He works with client teams on architecture, delivery discipline and the long term economics of data platforms, and stays involved in the engagements where the constraints are hardest.

His focus is simple: systems that can be explained, operated and audited years after they are built, by people who were not in the room when they were designed.

Umarani

Co-Founder

Umarani leads delivery and client relationships. She builds the teams that stay accountable after go-live, and holds the line on scope, documentation and handover.

Her work is centred on making sure the people who inherit a platform understand it well enough to change it without fear, and that client teams are never left guessing about the state of an engagement.
