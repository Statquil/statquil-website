Practice 03

Cloud services

Cloud should reduce operational drag, not add a second estate to manage. We build landing zones, move workloads and run cloud environments with guardrails that hold.

01 / The work

A foundation before a migration.

Most cloud problems start before the first workload lands: no account structure, no tagging standard, no network segmentation and no owner for the bill.

We fix the foundation first, then move at a pace the business can absorb. Migration is treated as an engineering program with checkpoints, not a one weekend event followed by a year of cleanup.

  • Cloud assessment with a cost baseline across accounts, workloads and existing commitments
  • Landing zone with account structure, identity, network segmentation, logging and guardrails
  • Migration waves with dependency mapping, cutover plans and tested rollback
  • Kubernetes and container platforms with sensible defaults, not a science project
  • Infrastructure as code for repeatable environments and reviewable change
  • CI/CD pipelines with environment promotion, approvals and release controls
  • Security baseline covering least privilege, encryption, key management and detection
  • FinOps with tagging, budgets, anomaly alerts and a monthly review owned by named teams

02 / Delivery

Assess, design, migrate, operate.

  1. 01

    Assess

    We map the existing estate, contracts and workload economics. The output is a factual baseline, including what should stay where it is.

  2. 02

    Design

    The landing zone is built before workloads move: identity, network, logging, guardrails and cost ownership. Migration waves are sequenced around dependencies, not vendor demos.

  3. 03

    Migrate

    Workloads move in controlled waves with parallel running, cutover checkpoints and a way back. Application teams know exactly what changes and when.

  4. 04

    Operate

    Environments are managed as code. Patching, backups, cost reviews and security findings run on a cadence, with evidence and named owners.

04 / Questions

What clients ask first.

Which cloud providers do you work with?

AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud, with the deepest bench in AWS and Azure. We also work in hybrid environments where part of the estate remains on-premises or in colocation.

Can you migrate without downtime?

For most workloads, yes. We use phased cutovers, replication and parallel running. Some systems cannot avoid a short maintenance window, and we will say so early rather than discover it on the night.

Do you resell cloud services?

No. We work on your accounts and your contracts. That keeps the incentive clean: our advice on commitment levels and architecture is not tied to a reseller margin.

How do you control cloud cost?

Cost is designed in before migration: account structure, tagging, budgets and unit economics per workload. After go-live, a monthly FinOps review looks at anomalies, waste and commitment coverage with the owners who can act.

Will you work alongside our platform team?

Yes. Many engagements are co-delivery. We bring architecture, automation and operating discipline, while your team keeps product context and long term ownership. Handover is part of the scope.
