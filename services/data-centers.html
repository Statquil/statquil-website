Practice 04

Data centers

Physical infrastructure is unforgiving. Power, cooling, cabling and access either work as designed or they do not. We plan, build, migrate and operate data center environments with that reality in view.

01 / The work

The physical layer has no undo.

A mislabeled cable, an overloaded circuit, a cooling setpoint nobody owns. In a data center, small mistakes are expensive and visible.

We plan and operate physical infrastructure with the same discipline as software: documented, tested, monitored and handed over with as-built records. The work covers client owned facilities and colocation environments, from a single rack to a multi site migration.

  • Requirements and site assessment across power, cooling, floor loading, connectivity and security
  • Colocation evaluation, technical due diligence and commercial negotiation support
  • Rack elevations, power budgets, cable schedules and labeling standards
  • Installation, commissioning and acceptance testing with witnessed results
  • Migration planning with dependency mapping, wave plans and tested rollback
  • Decommissioning with certified data destruction and asset disposal records
  • Operations covering preventive maintenance, environmental monitoring, access control and capacity tracking
  • As-built documentation and change records that match the room, not the intent

02 / Delivery

Survey, design, execute, operate.

  1. 01

    Survey

    We measure what exists: power capacity, cooling headroom, rack load, cabling, access paths and physical risk. Assumptions are replaced with readings and photographs.

  2. 02

    Design

    The target state is documented in rack elevations, power and cooling calculations, cable schedules and a migration sequence. Every change has an owner and a rollback.

  3. 03

    Execute

    Installation and migration run to a written plan with acceptance tests at each stage. Work is scheduled around business windows, not the other way around.

  4. 04

    Operate

    Environmental monitoring, preventive maintenance, access reviews and capacity tracking continue with as-built records updated on every change.

04 / Questions

What clients ask first.

Do you own or operate a data center?

No. Statquil is a technology services company. We design, migrate and operate environments in client owned and colocation facilities. We do not sell rack space. That independence matters when we advise you on where a workload should live.

Can you help select a colocation provider?

Yes. We turn your requirements into a technical scorecard covering power, cooling, connectivity, security, expansion and operational maturity. We then support site visits and commercial negotiation, and stay through commissioning.

Do you handle physical installation?

Yes. Rack and stack, structured cabling, labeling, power connections, commissioning and acceptance testing are delivered by our engineers, or supervised contractors where local regulations require it.

What happens at decommissioning?

Data bearing assets are destroyed through a certified process, with serial level records and certificates. Hardware is disposed of or resold under an agreed chain of custody. Nothing leaves the room unaccounted for.

Can you support remote sites?

Yes. Scheduled and on-call remote hands cover rack changes, cabling, hardware swaps, console access and escorted vendor visits. Every visit produces a written record with photographs.
