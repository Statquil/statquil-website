Services

Four practices, one accountable team.

Data warehousing, database management, cloud services and data centers. Separate disciplines, deliberately held to the same standard of delivery.

01 / The approach

The problems rarely stay inside one layer. So the accountability should not either.

A slow report is often a database problem. An unreliable pipeline is often a cloud permissions problem. A failing migration is often a physical cabling problem. Teams that hand these issues between vendors lose time the business does not have.

We keep the four practices under one delivery standard, with shared architecture review, documentation and escalation. You deal with one team, even when the work crosses four domains.

01

Data warehousing

Warehouses, lakehouses and semantic layers designed around the questions the business actually asks. We model for reconciliation and scale, not for the demo.

Typical work runs from a first warehouse build to a legacy migration, a lakehouse move or a performance rescue on a platform that has lost its shape.

Typical work

  • Warehouse and lakehouse builds
  • Legacy migration
  • Semantic layers
  • Performance tuning

02

Database management

Engine level care for the databases your applications cannot live without. Monitoring, recovery, upgrades and tuning handled by people who have been on call.

We take over existing estates, stabilize fragile ones and run them against written service levels. Both cloud managed and self hosted engines are in scope.

Typical work

  • Managed DBA
  • Backup and recovery
  • High availability
  • Upgrades and migrations

03

Cloud services

Landing zones, migrations and cloud operations with cost, security and reversibility designed in. Cloud should reduce operational drag, not add a second estate to manage.

Engagements cover assessment, landing zone design, workload migration, Kubernetes platforms, infrastructure as code and ongoing FinOps.

Typical work

  • Landing zones
  • Migration
  • Kubernetes platforms
  • FinOps and security

04

Data centers

Physical infrastructure planned, built and operated with the same rigor as the software inside it. Power, cooling, cabling and access either work as designed or they do not.

We support client owned facilities and colocation environments, from site selection and rack elevations to migrations, remote hands and decommissioning.

02 / Engagement models

Three ways to work with us.

Most engagements start with a short assessment. The model follows the problem, not the other way around.

01

Fixed scope delivery

A defined outcome, a written plan and a fixed price. Best for builds, migrations and remediation with a clear finish line.

02

Managed service

Ongoing responsibility for a platform or estate against agreed service levels. Includes monitoring, patching, response and reporting.

03

Embedded team

Senior engineers working inside your team, under your priorities. We bring the delivery discipline; you keep the direction.

03 / Getting started

A clear start, before any commitment.

No pitch deck. No discovery theatre. Three steps to a written point of view.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    Thirty minutes with the people who know the system. We ask about constraints, incidents and what has already been tried.

  2. 02

    Architecture review

    We look at the estate, the numbers and the run model. You receive an honest view of what should change and what should not.

  3. 03

    Written proposal

    Scope, sequence, staffing, assumptions and price. A document your finance and security teams can actually review.
