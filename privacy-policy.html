Information we collect

This policy explains how Statquil Technologies Private Limited handles personal information collected through statquil.com and direct correspondence. We collect only what is needed to respond, provide services and run our business.

Information you provide

If you email us or use the enquiry form, you may provide your name, company, email address, phone number and the content of your message. The form composes an email in your own mail application. It does not transmit or store your message on this website.

Technical information

Our hosting provider may record standard server information such as IP address, browser type, requested page and time of access. This is used for security, reliability and aggregate traffic understanding. We do not use it to build an advertising profile of you.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide requested information.
  • To assess and deliver services under a client agreement.
  • To process job applications and manage recruitment.
  • To meet legal, tax, audit and regulatory obligations.
  • To protect the website and our systems from misuse.

Lawful basis

Depending on the context, we process personal information on the basis of your consent, the performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, or our legitimate interests in running and protecting the business. Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it at any time.

Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell personal information. We may share it with service providers such as hosting and email providers who process it on our instructions, with professional advisers, or with authorities where required by law. We require service providers to apply appropriate confidentiality and security obligations.

We operate primarily in India. If a service provider processes information outside India, we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place as required by applicable law.

Retention and security

We retain correspondence for as long as needed to handle the enquiry, maintain the business relationship and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymize it. Candidate information is kept for twelve months unless you ask us not to keep it.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorized access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, erasure, withdrawal of consent, or raise a grievance. Write to contact@statquil.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Children

This website is intended for business use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies. It does not use third party analytics, advertising pixels or social tracking. Fonts, styles and images are served from this domain rather than an external network.

If this changes, this page will be updated before the change is deployed, and any required consent mechanism will be added.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or the law. The revised version takes effect when it is published on this page. Material changes affecting how we handle personal information will be highlighted on this page.
